Air Drum EEC 172 Final Project

An AWS and VR enabled Air Drum

Product Description

Our product is an air drum that a user can play with their hands or everyday objects. The drum allows you to play a snare and tom drum with your hands, as well as a kick and cymbal drum with a remote. You also have the ability to record your session, and when a recording is ended you will see the drum notation and your hits on an OLED. The recording will be sent to your email as well as be watchable through a VR interface on a Meta Quest.

The drum includes a snare drum and tom drum, as well as a kick drum and cymbal. The snare drum and tom drum are played utilizing two ultrasonic sensors, which are each associated with a drum. The ultrasonic sensors use an algorithm that determines the height in which you hit the drum, subtracting the initial height with the critical point, determining how hard you hit the drum and therefore how loud the drum should be. An IR sensor is used to play the kick drum and cymbal. The microcontroller decodes the IR sensor buttons and sends the associated sounds. These sounds are sent to a python script through UART that sends the sound to a speaker which plays the sound at its associated volume. A recording function is available utilizing the IR remote, recording which hits were played. When the recording is finished by the user, the drum notation notes are displayed on the OLED as well as a string representation of the hits that were made. The hits are sent using a POST request to AWS where they are sent to the users email as well as sent to an S3 bucket utilizing a lambda function. The user has the ability to retrieve their previous recording through a button on the remote that does a GET request to AWS. The S3 bucket is used by a C# script to display the hits recording on a VR headset in an Unity environment.
